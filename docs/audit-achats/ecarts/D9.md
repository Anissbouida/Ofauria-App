### G-D9-03 — Reçu non facturé / facturé non reçu / engagements : mesure fausse (en-tête) et incomplète — **P0**

- **Capacités / faiblesses** : C-D9-03 (essentiel), C-D8-02 (GR/IR), C-D6-04 ; W-10, W-15, W-19, W-49, W-04.
- **Statut** : present_defaillant.
- **Preuves** :
  - `server/src/repositories/dashboard.repository.ts:80-98` (carte) et `:172-192, 387-415` (liste / drill-down) : un BC est « reçu non facturé » s'il est `livre_complet|livre_partiel` **et** qu'aucune facture non annulée ne le référence (`NOT EXISTS … invoices / invoice_purchase_orders`). Un BC reçu 300 et facturé 100 n'apparaît pas ; un BC reçu 0 et facturé 2 500 (BC-2026-0036, W-15) non plus. Valorisation = `quantity_delivered × COALESCE(unit_price, 0)` du BC, alors que `unit_price` a été écrasé par la réception (`reception-voucher.repository.ts:385-396`) : ce n'est plus le prix commandé.
  - Le statut `en_attente_facturation` n'est pas dans la liste de statuts de la carte (`po.status IN ('livre_complet','livre_partiel')`).
  - Filtre période sur `po.delivery_date` (`:88`) alors que `delivery_date` est écrasée à chaque réception et NULL pour les BC passés `livre_complet` par édition (W-10, W-67).
  - SQL (en-tête, définition actuelle) :
    ```sql
    SELECT po.status, count(*), round(sum((SELECT COALESCE(SUM(quantity_delivered*COALESCE(unit_price,0)),0) FROM purchase_order_items WHERE purchase_order_id=po.id)),2)
    FROM purchase_orders po WHERE po.status IN ('livre_complet','livre_partiel','en_attente_facturation')
      AND NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM invoices i LEFT JOIN invoice_purchase_orders ipo ON ipo.invoice_id=i.id
                      WHERE (i.purchase_order_id=po.id OR ipo.purchase_order_id=po.id) AND i.invoice_type='received' AND i.status<>'cancelled')
    GROUP BY 1;
    -- en_attente_facturation | 1 | 0.00   (invisible de la carte)
    -- livre_complet          | 4 | 4346.20
    ```
  - SQL (par BC, reçu = Σ RVI × prix reçu, facturé = Σ `invoices.amount` HT, hors la facture fusionnée multi-BC) :
    ```sql
    WITH multi AS (SELECT invoice_id FROM invoice_purchase_orders GROUP BY 1 HAVING count(*)>1),
    recu AS (SELECT po.id, (SELECT COALESCE(SUM(rvi.quantity_received*COALESCE(rvi.unit_price,0)),0) FROM reception_vouchers rv JOIN reception_voucher_items rvi ON rvi.reception_voucher_id=rv.id WHERE rv.purchase_order_id=po.id) ht_recu,
                 (SELECT COALESCE(SUM(i.amount),0) FROM invoices i WHERE i.purchase_order_id=po.id AND i.invoice_type='received' AND i.status<>'cancelled' AND i.id NOT IN (SELECT invoice_id FROM multi)) ht_fact
          FROM purchase_orders po WHERE po.status<>'annule' AND NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM invoice_purchase_orders ipo WHERE ipo.purchase_order_id=po.id AND ipo.invoice_id IN (SELECT invoice_id FROM multi)))
    SELECT count(*) FILTER (WHERE ht_fact-ht_recu>0.5), round(sum(GREATEST(ht_fact-ht_recu,0)),2),
           count(*) FILTER (WHERE ht_recu-ht_fact>0.5), round(sum(GREATEST(ht_recu-ht_fact,0)),2) FROM recu;
    -- facturé > reçu : 29 BC, 14 669,26 DH HT   |  reçu > facturé : 7 BC, 780,11 DH
    SELECT count(*), round(sum(total_amount),2) FROM invoices i WHERE invoice_type='received' AND status<>'cancelled' AND purchase_order_id IS NOT NULL
      AND NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM reception_vouchers rv WHERE rv.purchase_order_id=i.purchase_order_id);
    -- 2 factures liées à un BC sans aucun BR : 7 348,00 DH
    SELECT po.status, count(DISTINCT po.id), round(sum((poi.quantity_ordered-poi.quantity_delivered)*COALESCE(poi.unit_price,0)),2)
    FROM purchase_orders po JOIN purchase_order_items poi ON poi.purchase_order_id=po.id WHERE po.status IN ('en_attente','envoye','livre_partiel') GROUP BY 1;
    -- envoye | 3 | 10 195,00  (engagement ouvert, affiché nulle part)
    SELECT count(*) FROM journal_entry_lines jel JOIN accounts a ON a.id=jel.account_id WHERE a.code='4417';  -- 0 (pas de GR/IR)
    ```

- **Manque vs standard ERP** : rapport de clôture n° 1 (ORA Uninvoiced Receipts, NS Accrued Purchase Receipts, ODOO Reçu non facturé) = par **ligne**, par fournisseur, par âge, valorisé au **prix commandé**, à une date `as_of` ; liste inverse (facturé sans réception) ; engagements = Σ (commandé − reçu − annulé) × prix.

    ```sql
                        SUM(rvi.quantity_received*COALESCE(rvi.unit_price,0)) amt_received, MAX(rv.reception_date) last_received_at

           GREATEST(COALESCE(inv.qty_invoiced,0)-COALESCE(rcv.qty_received,0),0) qty_invoiced_not_received,

    FROM purchase_order_items poi JOIN purchase_orders po ON po.id=poi.purchase_order_id
    LEFT JOIN ingredients ing ON ing.id=poi.ingredient_id LEFT JOIN packaging_items pkg ON pkg.id=poi.packaging_id
    LEFT JOIN rcv ON rcv.purchase_order_item_id=poi.id
    LEFT JOIN inv ON inv.purchase_order_id=po.id AND inv.item_id=COALESCE(poi.ingredient_id, poi.packaging_id)
    WHERE po.status<>'annule';

    CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW kpi_invoiced_not_received AS SELECT … WHERE qty_invoiced_not_received > 0;
    CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW kpi_open_commitments AS SELECT supplier_id, po_id, order_number, SUM(qty_open_commitment*COALESCE(price_po,0)) amount FROM v_po_line_match GROUP BY 1,2,3 HAVING SUM(qty_open_commitment)>0;
    ```
